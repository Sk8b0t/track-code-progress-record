Mary Kom also known as magnificent Mary , is India's most celebrated woman boxer
She made her Olympic debut in the 2012 London Olympics where women boxing was included for the 1st time
Competing in the 51kg category , she put up strong performances against her contenders
She advanced in the semi-finals and eventually won the bronze medal and becoming the 1st Indian Woman boxer to win an Olympic medal
This was a historic achievement . She dedicated her medal to the people of India and still remains inspiration for the upcoming women athletes
